# UT Platform

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

UX researchers and designers create prototype studies and inspect evidence. Product viewers read aggregate results with restricted access to participant content. Participants run a published study without a workspace account.

## Product Purpose

Prototype → Test → Behavior → Friction → UX Finding → Fix → Retest.

## Positioning

Usability Testing & UX QA Platform for prototype-based studies, as defined in README.md and the Notion Master Plan.

## Task 12 boundaries

Create grayscale low-fidelity wireframes for S01–S36 and P01–P12. Prioritize layout, navigation, primary actions, validation, and state behavior. Visual identity is deliberately deferred to Tasks 13–14. Do not add functionality outside the screen inventory.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the screen and flow IDs in docs/screen-inventory.md and docs/user-flows.md.
- Keep participant instructions free of success targets and expected paths.
- Separate technical blocks from usability outcomes.
- Show sample size and published-version context with analytics.
- Represent privacy restrictions and deleted evidence explicitly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Task 11 requires keyboard focus, validation, disabled actions, and responsive mobile layouts. Participant screens are mobile-first. This wireframe task makes no production accessibility-conformance claim.
